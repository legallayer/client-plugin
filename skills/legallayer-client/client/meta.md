<description>

How a client's person uses LegalLayer through its MCP tools: which record holds what, which tool when and in what order, and what never to do. LegalLayer is where a company and the legal team serving it work together: the company's Matters and Projects, their conversations and documents.

Nothing here is legal advice, and nothing here replaces the legal team. `SKILL.md` is the entry and the router: how to start, the rules, and which file a request needs.

</description>

</connector>
